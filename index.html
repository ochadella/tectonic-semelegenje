The SUN Strategy

Three strategies, one connected funnel

Mapped to the AARRR framework and the three problems in the casebook: acquisition, retention, and conversion.

S

Spark with AI

Acquisition & Activation
  • AI Mini Test with a personal learning profile
  • Seasonal campaigns (LPDP, UTBK, CPNS) with KOLs (Key Opinion Leaders)
  • Migration of 10,000+ web users
1M downloads40% activation
U

Unlock Daily Habits

Retention & Referral
  • Daily habit loop (Hook Model): trigger, action, reward, investment
  • AI Weakness Map + 5-minute Kelas Kilat
  • Streaks & lives rewarded with AI Credits; Fighter League
  • Cross-exam journey: UTBK → CPNS → LPDP
25% MAUD7 25% / D30 15%≥20% cross-exam
N

Nurture to Premium

Revenue
  • Contextual paywall at high-intent moments
  • Clear freemium boundary + Season Pass until exam day
  • Terang Expert Connect: paid expert interview sessions
  • Impactful Premium: cross-subsidy for 3T students
